Privacy Policy

1. Client-Side Processing & Data Security

ToolLab.org ("we", "our", or "us") operates on a 100% client-side architecture. All file conversions, text formatting, calculations, and utility routines execute entirely within your browser's local sandbox memory using HTML5, JavaScript, and WebAssembly. We do not upload, collect, or store your personal documents, images, code snippets, or financial data on any remote servers.

2. Analytics and Traffic Measurement

To improve our web utilities, we use Google Analytics (GA4) to collect anonymous usage statistics, such as page views and general interaction events. IP anonymization is enabled to protect user privacy. These analytics help us identify which tools are most useful for our global audience.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

ToolLab does not use intrusive tracking cookies. Local storage may be utilized solely to preserve user preferences (such as tool input states or UI configurations) locally on your device.

4. Third-Party Links

Our platform may contain links to external sites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of such third-party websites.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via our GitHub repository or project support channels.
