Calendar & Working Day Tracking Rules

1. Calendar Days vs. Business Days

Calendar days represent elapsed consecutive 24-hour periods. Business days exclude Saturdays and Sundays to measure real productivity capacity for commercial projects.

2. D-Day Notation Standards

D-Day (D-0): The day of the designated milestone.
D-N: Exactly N days remaining until the target arrives.
D+N: Exactly N days have passed since the event occurred.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Are my saved dates or event titles stored on a server?

Never. All inputs execute locally inside your browser runtime. Zero scheduling information is ever dispatched to external servers.

Q. Does the business day calculation include public holidays?

This client-side engine strips weekends (Saturday and Sunday). Because statutory public holidays differ by country and region, local bank holidays must be adjusted manually.
