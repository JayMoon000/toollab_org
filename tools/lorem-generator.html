Why Typesetters Use Lorem Ipsum

1. Neutral Visual Weight Distribution

Using readable English copy during UI design reviews distracts stakeholders into reading the copy rather than assessing font pairings, kerning, line heights, and visual hierarchy. Lorem Ipsum provides a natural, semi-realistic character distribution without readable distraction.

2. Varied Word Lengths

Unlike repeated words (e.g. "Content here, content here"), Latin dummy text contains a diverse spread of letter lengths, simulating realistic typographic rhythm across desktop and mobile screens.
