The Corner Anchoring Strategy

  • Anchor Highest Tile: Keep your largest number locked permanently in one corner (e.g., bottom-right). Never swipe in the opposite direction (Up) unless completely forced.
  • Descending Snake Chain: Order surrounding tiles in strict descending sequence (e.g., 1024 → 512 → 256 → 128) along the bottom row to trigger cascading merges.
  • Keep 4+ Free Cells: When free cells fall below 3, immediately merge smaller numbers (2s and 4s) to prevent gridlock.
